I'm a Ph.D. student at the State Key Laboratory of Marine Environmental Science, Xiamen University, working with Prof. Fei Chai on ocean biogeochemical modeling.

I completed my Master's in Physical Oceanology at the Institute of Oceanology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (2022–2025), and my Bachelor's in Ocean Engineering and Technology at China University of Geosciences, Wuhan (2018–2022).

My research centers on coupling the CoSiNE ecosystem model with CESM. I've been extending CoSiNE16 from 16 to 27 tracers by incorporating carbon isotopes (¹³C, ¹⁴C), the microbial carbon pump, and iron cycling — enabling the model to reproduce signals recorded in geological archives such as foraminiferal δ¹³C and Δ¹⁴C.

Research Focus

研究方向

🌊 CoSiNE Model DevelopmentCoSiNE 模型开发

CoSiNE is an ocean ecosystem model that simulates interactions among nutrients, plankton, and carbon in the marine environment. Originally developed by Prof. Fei Chai's group (Chai et al., 2002), my work extends it for paleoclimate applications.

The approach involves integrating CoSiNE16 into CESM2.1.5's ocean component (POP2), replacing the default MARBL/ecosys module, and progressively adding new capabilities — carbon isotopes, microbial carbon pump, and iron cycling — until the model reaches 27 tracers capable of simulating signals preserved in geological archives.

Key Research Topics

① Coupling — Integrating CoSiNE as a passive tracer module within CESM's ocean framework.

② Carbon Isotopes — Tracking ¹³C and ¹⁴C through the ocean carbon cycle to enable comparison with foraminiferal proxy records.

③ Microbial Carbon Pump — Parameterizing the conversion of labile organic carbon to refractory DOC, with a residence time of thousands of years in the deep ocean.

④ Iron Cycle — Adding dissolved and particulate iron dynamics to capture nutrient limitation in HNLC regions.

CoSiNE 是一个海洋生态系统模型，模拟海洋中营养盐、浮游生物与碳之间 的相互作用。该模型最初由柴扉教授团队开发（Chai et al., 2002）， 我的工作是将其扩展以服务于古气候研究。

基本思路是将 CoSiNE16 接入 CESM2.1.5 的海洋分量 POP2，替换默认的 MARBL/ecosys 模块，然后逐步加入碳同位素、微生物碳泵和铁循环等新功能， 最终将示踪物扩展至 27 个，使模型能够重现地质记录中保存的信号。

主要研究方向

① 模型耦合 — 将 CoSiNE 以被动示踪物模块形式集成到 CESM 的海洋框架中。

② 碳同位素 — 在海洋碳循环中追踪 ¹³C 和 ¹⁴C， 以实现与有孔虫代用指标的直接对比。

③ 微生物碳泵 — 参数化活性有机碳向惰性 DOC 的转化过程， 其在深海的停留时间可达数千年。

④ 铁循环 — 加入溶解铁和颗粒铁动力学，捕捉 HNLC 海域 的营养盐限制效应。
