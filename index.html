I'm a Ph.D. student at Xiamen University's State Key Laboratory of Marine Environmental Science, working with Prof. Fei Chai on ocean biogeochemical modeling.

Before coming to Xiamen, I did my Master's at the Institute of Oceanology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (2022–2025), and my Bachelor's at China University of Geosciences in Wuhan (2018–2022), where I studied ocean engineering and technology.

My current work is about making the CoSiNE ecosystem model talk to CESM — essentially teaching a global climate model to better simulate how carbon moves through the ocean. I've been extending CoSiNE16 from 16 to 27 tracers by adding carbon isotopes, the microbial carbon pump, and iron cycling, which lets us compare model output directly with geological records like foraminiferal δ¹³C and Δ¹⁴C.

Research Focus

研究方向

🌊 CoSiNE Model DevelopmentCoSiNE 模型开发

CoSiNE is an ocean ecosystem model that simulates how nutrients, plankton, and carbon interact in the ocean. It was built by Prof. Fei Chai's group (Chai et al., 2002), and I'm helping extend it for climate research.

The basic idea: I take CoSiNE16 (with its 16 tracers for nutrients, phytoplankton, zooplankton, and detritus) and plug it into CESM2.1.5, replacing the default marine biology module. Then I add new capabilities — carbon isotopes, microbial carbon pump, iron cycle — until we have 27 tracers that can simulate what geologists actually measure in sediment cores.

What I'm Working On

① Coupling — Getting CoSiNE to run inside CESM's ocean component (POP2) as a passive tracer module.

② Carbon Isotopes — Tracking ¹³C and ¹⁴C through the ocean so we can compare model output with foraminiferal proxy records.

③ Microbial Carbon Pump — Modeling how bacteria turn labile organic carbon into refractory DOC that stays in the deep ocean for thousands of years.

④ Iron Cycle — Adding iron dynamics to capture nutrient limitation in regions like the Southern Ocean.

CoSiNE 是一个海洋生态系统模型，用来模拟海洋里营养盐、浮游生物和碳 之间的相互作用。它是柴扉教授团队开发的（Chai et al., 2002），我在做的 是帮它升级，让它能用于气候研究。

基本思路是：把 CoSiNE16（16 个示踪物，涵盖营养盐、浮游植物、浮游动物 和碎屑）接入 CESM2.1.5，替换掉默认的海洋生物模块。然后逐步加入碳同位素、 微生物碳泵、铁循环，最终扩展到 27 个示踪物，让模型能模拟地质学家在沉积 岩芯里实际测量的那些指标。

目前在做的事

① 模型耦合 — 让 CoSiNE 在 CESM 的海洋分量 POP2 里 以被动示踪物模块的形式运行起来。

② 碳同位素 — 在海洋中追踪 ¹³C 和 ¹⁴C，这样模型输出 就能和有孔虫代用指标直接对比。

③ 微生物碳泵 — 模拟细菌如何把活性有机碳转化成惰性 DOC，这些碳会在深海里待上几千年。

④ 铁循环 — 加入铁动力学，捕捉南大洋这类海域的 营养盐限制效应。
