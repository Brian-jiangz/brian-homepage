I am a Ph.D. candidate at the College of Ocean and Earth Sciences, Xiamen University. My research focuses on Earth System Modeling, specifically the coupling of the Community Earth System Model (CESM) with the Carbon, Silicate, Nitrogen Ecosystem (CoSiNE) model to better understand marine biogeochemical processes and their interactions with the climate system.

My work aims to improve the representation of carbon and nitrogen cycling in ocean ecosystem models, contributing to more accurate climate projections and a deeper understanding of the ocean's role in the global carbon cycle.

Research Focus

🌊 CESM-CoSiNE Coupling

The core of my doctoral research: coupling the Community Earth System Model (CESM) with the CoSiNE (Carbon, Silicate, Nitrogen Ecosystem) biogeochemical module. This coupled framework enables comprehensive simulation of marine ecosystem dynamics, including phytoplankton growth, nutrient cycling, and carbon export in the ocean.

Key Objectives

① Model Coupling — Integrating CoSiNE into CESM's ocean component (POP/MPAS) to replace or complement the default marine biogeochemistry module.

② Parameter Optimization — Calibrating ecosystem parameters against satellite observations (SeaWiFS, MODIS) and in-situ datasets (BGC-Argo, GEOTRACES).

③ Process Understanding — Investigating how nutrient limitation (N, Si, Fe) controls primary production and the biological carbon pump across different ocean basins.

④ Climate Sensitivity — Assessing how marine biogeochemical feedbacks influence climate projections under various emission scenarios (SSP).
